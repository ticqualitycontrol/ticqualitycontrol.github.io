What is Egypt CoC?

The Egypt CoC stands for Certificate of Conformity, a mandatory document confirming that exported products meet Egyptian quality and safety standards.

Under the Egypt CoC program, products are verified before shipment to ensure compliance with Egyptian regulations. This certification helps reduce risks associated with non-compliant imports while protecting consumers and improving trade quality.

Upper Gulf Shipping Reopens: New Routing for Exporters to Iraq and UAE

Upper Gulf shipping has resumed, bringing important updates for exporters targeting Iraq, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, and Qatar. Hapag-Lloyd AG has restarted bookings using alternative routing solutions while avoiding direct transit via the Strait of Hormuz.

What Has Changed in Upper Gulf Shipping?

Instead of direct shipping routes, cargo is now moving through transshipment hubs like Sharjah. Shipments are transferred via feeder vessels and bonded trucking between Sharjah and Khorfakkan, adding flexibility to Upper Gulf shipping operations.

Impact on Exporters to Iraq and GCC Countries

Exporters should expect slightly longer transit times, variable schedules, and additional coordination. Shipping to Iraq now requires careful planning, especially with compliance requirements such as Certificate of Conformity (CoC) and inspection processes.

Adapting to New Logistics Conditions

Businesses involved in Upper Gulf shipping must focus on flexible supply chain strategies, accurate documentation, and reliable logistics partners. These changes highlight the importance of risk management in modern global trade.
