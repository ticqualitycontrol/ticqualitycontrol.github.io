What is Iraq CoC?

Iraq CoC refers to the Certificate of Conformity required for many products exported to Iraq. The objective of the Iraq CoC program is to ensure that imported goods comply with relevant technical regulations, safety requirements, and quality standards.

An Iraq CoC Certificate serves as documented evidence that products have undergone conformity assessment procedures before shipment. This documentation is frequently required during customs clearance and supports smooth import procedures.

Understanding the Iraq CoC Program

The Iraq CoC program was established to strengthen product quality, safety, and regulatory compliance across imported goods entering Iraq. Through inspections, testing, document review, and conformity verification, the Iraq CoC program helps ensure that products meet applicable standards.

Companies that understand Iraq CoC requirements early in the export process are often better positioned to avoid shipment delays and customs-related complications.
