What is Iraq CoC? Understanding Certificate of Conformity for Iraq

The Iraq Certificate of Conformity (Iraq CoC) is an official document confirming that products meet Iraqi technical standards and regulatory requirements before import. The Iraq CoC is issued by authorized inspection bodies following Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI), testing, and documentary assessment at the country of origin.

Iraq operates a mandatory Conformity Assessment Program (CAP) that came into effect in January 2020. Under this program, a Certificate of Conformity for Iraq is required for a comprehensive range of regulated product categories before goods can clear Iraqi customs. Without a valid Iraq CoC certificate, shipments are rejected, returned, or subjected to costly detention at Iraqi ports.

The Certificate of Conformity for Iraq is implemented through COSQC (Central Organization for Standardization and Quality Control), Iraq's official standards body. COSQC appoints international inspection and certification bodies such as TIC Quality Control (TICQC) to conduct Iraq CoC certification and issue valid Iraq Certificate of Conformity documents on COSQC's behalf.

COSQC and Iraq CoC Program Overview

The Central Organization for Standardization and Quality Control (COSQC) is Iraq's official standards authority responsible for regulating imported goods and ensuring product compliance. COSQC sets Iraq CoC policy, updates regulations, and appoints international bodies to conduct Iraq Certificate of Conformity assessments and issue Iraq CoC certificates.

Iraq's Conformity Assessment Program reflects the country's commitment to protecting domestic industries, ensuring consumer safety, and preventing non-compliant goods from flooding Iraqi markets. The Iraq CoC requirement applies across numerous product categories and is enforced rigorously at Iraqi ports.

Only COSQC-appointed bodies can issue Iraq CoC certificates that Iraqi customs will recognize. Using unauthorized inspection companies for your Iraq Certificate of Conformity will result in rejection, leaving you no choice but to obtain certification from an authorized body before your shipment can proceed.

The Iraq CoC Certification Process: Step-by-Step

The Iraq Certificate of Conformity process follows a structured pathway. Understanding each step helps you plan timelines and budget accurately for your Iraq CoC certification.

Step 1: Request for Certification (RFC)

Submit a formal Request for Certification to your authorized Iraq CoC provider. Include complete product descriptions, manufacturer information, intended importer details, country of origin, and applicable Iraq standards for your goods.

Step 2: Documentary Review and Assessment

COSQC-appointed bodies review your product documentation as part of the Iraq CoC process. This includes commercial invoices, packing lists, product specifications, existing test reports from accredited laboratories, manufacturing certifications, product labels, and safety documentation. Documentary review of your Iraq Certificate of Conformity application identifies gaps before physical inspection.

Step 3: Laboratory Testing (Where Required)

If your Iraq CoC application lacks valid test reports from accredited laboratories, product samples are tested against applicable Iraqi standards. This testing phase of your Iraq Certificate of Conformity process ensures products meet performance requirements before inspection.

Step 4: Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI)

An authorized inspector conducts Pre-Shipment Inspection at your facility. The Iraq CoC inspection includes visual examination of goods, verification of product quantities and packing, checking labels and markings for Iraq standard compliance, and sampling as required. The PSI is the critical physical verification phase of your Iraq CoC certification process.

Step 5: Certificate of Conformity Issuance

Upon successful completion of documentary review, testing, and inspection, your COSQC-appointed Iraq CoC provider issues the official Iraq Certificate of Conformity. The Iraq CoC certificate is transmitted to COSQC's system and provided to you for inclusion in shipping documents. Your Iraq CoC authorization permits customs clearance.

Step 6: Iraqi Border Inspection and Clearance

Upon arrival in Iraq, your shipment undergoes border inspection. A valid Iraq CoC certificate from an authorized body significantly expedites this process and helps ensure smooth entry into Iraqi markets.

Different product categories have specific Iraq CoC requirements within the broader certification framework.

Toys: Iraq CoC requirements for toys are stringent, involving testing for mechanical hazards, small parts risks, chemical composition analysis, flammability assessment, and electrical safety for electronic toys. An Iraq Certificate of Conformity for toys must reference relevant international toy safety standards.

Construction Materials: Iraq CoC for building materials requires testing of mechanical properties, dimensional tolerances, chemical composition, and structural integrity. Your Iraq Certificate of Conformity documentation must reference applicable construction standards.

Chemical Products: Chemicals subject to Iraq CoC certification require Safety Data Sheets, hazard classifications, proper labelling, and transport documentation. Iraq CoC certificates for chemicals must include full chemical composition and handling requirements.

Common Mistakes in Iraq CoC Certification

Avoiding these frequent errors prevents delays and rejection of your Iraq Certificate of Conformity:

Incomplete Documentation: Missing invoices, specifications, or test reports delay Iraq CoC certification. Prepare comprehensive documentation before submitting your request.

Non-Accredited Test Reports: Test reports from non-accredited laboratories are rejected during Iraq CoC assessment. Always use ISO/IEC 17025-accredited testing facilities for your Iraq Certificate of Conformity application.

Outdated Standards Reference: Using outdated Iraqi standards in your Iraq CoC application causes rejection. Verify current applicable standards before starting your Iraq Certificate of Conformity process.

Label Non-Compliance: Products must carry labels in Arabic with required safety information, country of origin, manufacturer details, and technical specifications. Non-compliant labelling is a frequent Iraq CoC rejection reason.

Product Mismatches: If goods inspected during Iraq CoC Pre-Shipment Inspection differ from documented specifications, non-conformity is raised. Maintain strict production consistency for Iraq Certificate of Conformity.

Iraq CoC and Iraq VoC: Understanding the Program

Iraq's Verification of Conformity (VoC) program is the formal name for Iraq's mandatory certification system. The Iraq CoC is the practical outcome of this program. Frequently Asked Questions About Iraq CoC

Is Iraq CoC mandatory for all product categories? No. Only COSQC-regulated products require an Iraq Certificate of Conformity. Confirm your specific product category before planning shipment.

Can Iraq CoC be obtained after goods ship? The Iraq CoC program is pre-shipment by design. Post-shipment certification creates complications and potential rejection. Always obtain Iraq Certificate of Conformity before shipment.

How long is Iraq CoC valid? Iraq CoC certificates are typically shipment-specific. Each new shipment requires a fresh Iraq Certificate of Conformity. Some products may qualify for registration allowing simplified certification for subsequent shipments.

What happens if Iraq CoC is rejected at customs? Shipments without valid Iraq CoC or with rejected certificates are held at port, incurring storage fees, and must either be returned or re-certified before clearance.
