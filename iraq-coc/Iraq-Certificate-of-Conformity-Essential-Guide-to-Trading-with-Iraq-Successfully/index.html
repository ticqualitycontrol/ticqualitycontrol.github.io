What is an Iraq Certificate of Conformity (CoC)?

An Iraq Certificate of Conformity (Iraq CoC) is an official document confirming that products meet the quality, safety, and regulatory standards required by Iraqi authorities. The Iraq CoC serves as proof that your goods comply with Iraq's import regulations and local quality standards. This certification is crucial for any business seeking to import products into Iraq or trade with Iraqi partners.

The Iraq Certificate of Conformity process involves rigorous testing and documentation to ensure products meet international and local standards. TICQC has extensive experience issuing the Iraq CoC across various product categories, from industrial goods to consumer products.
